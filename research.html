Research

I work on quantitative MRI: building acquisition and reconstruction methods that turn MR signals into maps of tissue properties.

Focus areas

Quantitative T1 mapping

T1 maps report a physical property of tissue rather than a relative image contrast, which makes them comparable across scans and sites. I work on fast 3D T1 mapping using accelerated stack-of-spirals acquisitions.

Pulse sequence design

Designing and tuning sequences on clinical scanners so that the acquisition is efficient, and so that the signal model used for fitting matches what the scanner actually does.

Accelerated reconstruction

Parallel imaging (SENSE) and compressed sensing (CS-SENSE) reconstruction to shorten scan time while keeping the quantitative maps accurate.

Magnetization transfer modeling

Accounting for magnetization transfer effects in the signal model, since they can bias T1 estimates if ignored.
